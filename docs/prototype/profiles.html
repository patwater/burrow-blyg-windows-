How it works

Open a profile from anywhereAn author's address in the reading header, the "↳ stub of" / "⑂ forked from" line, the source line under a quote, a mention, an entry in someone's blogroll, or a pasted URL (⇧⌘O Open profile…). An RSS feed gets a simpler card: title, link and recent posts.
What a profile showsName, bio, avatar and links from their manifest. Blogroll: their public blogroll.opml, each entry with Follow / Following. Posts: recent items from their archive index. Connections: blygs they quote, stub or fork, taken from their own posts. Lists only, no counts.
Follow = subscribeFollow runs the same subscribe preview you already have, in one click. Following is private and client-local, so nobody gets a follower count. "Add to my blogroll" is a separate, deliberate choice. Profiles are cached and work offline.

Worth deciding

PrivacyOpening a profile fetches public files from that person's server, so it sees your IP, just like visiting their site in a browser. The app never sends your token or cookies. Option: fetch only when you open a profile, never in the background (proposed).
Connections tabBuilt from the posts of theirs you hold, plus their archive index. Useful for "A stubs B, B looks interesting". It can be left out of v1 if it feels noisy.
Your own profileThe same sheet for your blyg (⌘I on your post) shows what visitors see, with "Edit site settings…" and your blogroll with toggles.
